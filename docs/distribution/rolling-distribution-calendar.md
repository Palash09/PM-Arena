# Product Decision League: Rolling Distribution Calendar

Prepared August 19, 2026. The daily briefing runs at 11:00 AM ET. Publishing and external interactions require explicit approval and remain manual unless the user approves a supported connected tool at the time of action.

## Operating rules

- Use one primary distribution action per day. Do not turn every day into a product promotion.
- Prefer 11:15 AM ET / 8:45 PM IST for mixed US-India posts. Use 4:00 PM ET for US-first follow-ups.
- Put product links in owned posts only. In replies and communities, lead with a useful point and share a link only when requested and permitted.
- Recheck Reddit and community rules immediately before posting.
- Ask: "What felt least credible or most confusing?" during the alpha.
- Review challenge starts, completions, CTA clicks, account creation, and second-scenario activation before selecting the next promotional post.
- Never publish, schedule, tag, message, or comment from an external account without explicit approval.

## Daily briefing format

Every briefing should contain:

1. One primary action and why it is today's priority.
2. Recommended time in ET and IST.
3. Complete paste-ready copy or a complete value-first response.
4. Exact tracked URL, media, and target community when applicable.
5. A 10-minute follow-up checklist.
6. One metric to inspect the next day.
7. A short preview confirming that tomorrow's content is ready.
8. A clear statement that no external action will occur without approval.

## Publishing ownership

LinkedIn, X, Substack, and Reddit publishing is not delegated by this calendar. The daily briefing may prepare content and assess available connected tools. The user either publishes manually or gives explicit approval for a supported tool to perform the specific action. Approval for one post does not authorize future posts.
